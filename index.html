How to use this guide

This is a complete facilitator training that works two ways. If you are attending a live training, follow along with your facilitator and use the modules to revisit anything you want to go deeper on. If you are learning on your own, the modules are written to guide you through each idea at your own pace — read the “Going deeper” sections, try the self-study notes, and complete each checkpoint before moving on.

1 · LearnWork through the six modules in order. Each builds the mindset, evidence, and safety you need.
2 · PracticeOpen the five activity guides. Use them after the modules or during a real session.
3 · PrepareSet up your Toolkit: care boundaries, local support contacts, and the glossary.
4 · ReflectCommit to one small first session, then record what worked and what you would adjust.
